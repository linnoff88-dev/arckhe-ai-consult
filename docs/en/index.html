What the AI Strategy Factory is

It’s a tool that automatically does the work of an AI consulting firm. What would normally cost weeks and thousands of reais—assessment, roadmap, ROI analysis, usage policy, training plan—it delivers in minutes, for pennies. You only need the company name.

How it works — 3 phases

A simple funnel, from the name to the finished package.

  • 1️⃣Research — Perplexity searches for real data about the company and its industry
  • 2️⃣Summary — Gemini writes the 15 strategic documents
  • 3️⃣Generation — the program builds PPTX, DOCX, and diagrams on your computer

What you get

A complete package, ready to present.

  • 📄15 documents — assessment, roadmap, ROI, governance, prompts
  • 📊2 presentations — executive summary + full report (PPTX)
  • 📝2 Word reports — final strategy + work proposal
  • 📐Diagrams — current and future architecture and roadmap (PNG)

What it really costs

A complete report costs between US$ 0,05 e US$ 0,90 in AI calls — the price of a coffee for what a consulting firm would charge thousands for.

Quick mode: ~US$ 0,05. In-depth mode: ~US$ 0,50. You decide how deep to go.

Who this course is for

For anyone who wants to generate AI strategies without being a programmer: consultants, founders, innovation teams, teachers, and curious learners. You don’t need to know how to code — just follow the steps. Everything is explained in Portuguese, from start to finish.

Quick start

From zero to your first report in 6 steps. (Each step is detailed in Track 1.)

1

Download the project

Clone the repository github.com/inematds/AI-CONSULT and open the folder in the terminal.

2

Get the 2 API keys (free)

One from Perplexity and one from Google Gemini. Takes ~10 minutes total.

3

Install with python setup.py

The script creates the environment and installs everything automatically. There’s also a manual walkthrough if you prefer.

4

Paste the keys into the file .env

Open the file in a text editor and paste in the two keys. It’s the only file you need to edit.

5

Open the app with python -m strategy_factory.webapp

The browser opens automatically at localhost:8888. From here, just point and click.

6

Enter the company name and download the package

Enter the company name, click Start, wait a few minutes, and download all the files.
